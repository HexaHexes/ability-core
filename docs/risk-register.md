# Risk register (REQ-DOC-03)

**Review rule: no risk without an evidence link.** Every entry carries
owner, trigger, mitigation and status; statuses are `managed` (mitigation
applies today), `open` (action outstanding), `open limitation` (accepted
constraint) or `deferred` (explicitly parked with a tracker). The register is
**reviewed at each release** - the checklist step lives in
[`docs/release-plan.md`](release-plan.md).

Seeded from the investigation spikes and the DEC register
(`docs/requirements.md`) on 2026-11-05.
